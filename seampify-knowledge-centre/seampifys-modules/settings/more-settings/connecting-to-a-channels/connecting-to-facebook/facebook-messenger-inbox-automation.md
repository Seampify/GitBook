# Facebook Messenger Inbox Automation

Inbox Automation allows you to auto reply to your customers in messenger. This will allow you to save your time and to allow customers to know when you are away.

### Pre-requisite before we continue.

To create a Facebook Messenger Inbox automation, you need to ensure that you are a Facebook Page admin, editor or moderator.

### How to setup auto-reply on Facebook Messenger

Step 1: To get started, you will need to go to your [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/latest/home). You can access the automation settings by pressing **Inbox**.\
![](<../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (56).png>)

Step 2: Next, press **Automation**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (57).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 3: Press to **Create Automation**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (58).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 4: Choose **Instant Reply**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (59).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 5: Setup your Auto-Reply

First, turn on **Instant Reply**.![](<../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (60).png>)

Next, select **Messenger**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (61).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Lastly, set up your message and press Save Changes.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (62).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
