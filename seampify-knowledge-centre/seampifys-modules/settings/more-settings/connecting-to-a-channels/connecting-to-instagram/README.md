# Connecting to Instagram

## **Pre-Requisites**

Before proceeding, ensure the following:

1. **Switch to Instagram Professional Account:** Convert your Instagram account to a Professional account. Refer to [Instagram’s guide](setting-up-instagram-for-channel-integration.md#switch-instagram-personal-to-business) for instructions.
2. **Connect Instagram to a Facebook Page:** Link your Instagram account to a Facebook Page by following [Facebook’s guide](setting-up-instagram-for-channel-integration.md#connect-instagram-to-a-facebook-page).
3. **Enable Message Control Connected Tools:** Allow message access for connected tools. Visit the [guide](setting-up-instagram-for-channel-integration.md#allow-access-to-messages-in-instagram) for more details.
4. **Ensure you have Admin Access role.**

***

## **Steps to Connect Instagram Channels**

### **1. Access Channels in Seampify:**

* Navigate to **Settings > Channels** in your Seampify account.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (807).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **2. Create an Instagram Channel:**

* Click **Create Channel.**
* Select **Instagram** and then click **Next.**

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (822).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **3. Connect Instagram Page:**

* Click **Connect Instagram Page** to begin the integration.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (821).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **4. Log In to Facebook:**

* A new tab will open. Enter the login credentials of the Facebook account linked to your Instagram Business account.
* Continue with the logged-in account.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (817).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **5. Select Instagram Business Account:**

* After logging in, it will brings you back to Seampify and you are able to choose the Instagram Page you wish to connect and click **Finish** to complete the channel creation.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (820).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
You will only be able to select Pages that you have access to.\
If you can't find the Page you want to connect, please check if you have access to it in your Meta Business Portfolio.
{% endhint %}



## **Your Instagram Channel is Ready**

Once completed, you will see your Seampify Instagram Channel listed in the Channels section, ready to manage customer interactions seamlessly.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (823).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
