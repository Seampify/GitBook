# Setting Up Instagram for Channel Integration

## **Switch Instagram Personal to Business**

An Instagram Business Account is required to create an Instagram Channel in Seampify. Follow these steps to convert your personal account to a business account:

1. First, open your Instagram application, then go to your profile next to likes (bottom right corner.)
2. In the profile, tap on ![Menu](https://docs.rampwin.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic.xx.fbcdn.net%2Fassets%2F%3Frevision%3D424874742891505%26name%3Dinstagram-sidetrayhamburger-ios%26density%3D1\&width=40\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=44c1d65\&sv=2) (top right corner.)
3. A menu will appear; in it, select **Settings.** ![](https://docs.rampwin.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FhElFPtMZjXYjDDMBT5q2%252Fuploads%252FJy3HZXhPB0OaPai9517P%252FSettings%2520Instagram.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3De2d1dc5a-438a-4f5f-8d54-57d3ee8aecf4\&width=40\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=3a2af2e3\&sv=2)
4. Then, choose **Account**. ![](https://docs.rampwin.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FhElFPtMZjXYjDDMBT5q2%252Fuploads%252F3hWCPioYEbdoMX3zCXJN%252FInstagram%2520Account%2520Logo.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D2dbae2da-1c6f-4506-bece-30be261785d4\&width=40\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=46ab1b5f\&sv=2)
5. Scroll down and selec&#x74;**, Switch to professional account**.
6. Tap **Continue.**
7. Search your business category or select from the given. Once selected, tap on **Done.**
8. It will take a few moments to process your account. Once the process is completed, select **Business** and tap on **Next**.
9. On the next screen, add your contact details and tap on **Next.**
10. The next screen will ask you to [connect to a Facebook Page](setting-up-instagram-for-channel-integration.md#connect-instagram-to-a-facebook-page). You should consider doing that because it will be required to create a Seampify Instagram Channel.



## Connect Instagram to a Facebook Page

For Seampify Channel to work, you need to connect your Instagram Business Account to a Facebook. The following guide will assist you to do that.

1. Login to your Facebook account, then from your Feed, go to **Pages**![](https://docs.rampwin.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic.xx.fbcdn.net%2Fassets%2F%3Frevision%3D424874742891505%26name%3Dfb-pageflag-desktop%26density%3D1\&width=300\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=e7908b05\&sv=2) in the left menu.
2. From the page list, select the page you want to connect.
3. Then, go to your Left Tab/Menu, scroll down and select **Settings**![](https://docs.rampwin.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic.xx.fbcdn.net%2Fassets%2F%3Frevision%3D424874742891505%26name%3Dfb-settings-comet%26density%3D1\&width=300\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=d48c02c5\&sv=2).
4. Once you're in the settings, click **Instagram**, then further click **Connect Account**.
5. Set **Allow access to Instagram messages in Inbox** to ON.
6. Then, click **Confirm**.
7. After this, enter your username and password and click **Log In**.
8. Click **Connect account**, then click **Confirm**.
