# Personal Settings

There are 2 ways to access Personal Settings:

* From the Left Sidebar Settings modules&#x20;
* Or, click on your profile icon, then your profile

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (208).png" alt="" width="193"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



The **Personal Setting** have 3 sub-menus: My Profile, Security & Password and Notifications. You and your team members will manage these different settings for their account themselves without confusion.



## My Profile

Here, you can update your **Profile Picture**, **Name** and **Email Address**. The name displayed here will be used to identify who sent the chat message. You also have the option to show this name to your contact when a message is sent manually by your user account.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (209).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



## Security & Password

Here, you can reset your password by entering your old password and then setting a new one.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (210).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Ensure the new password following these guidelines:

* <mark style="color:red;">Use alphanumeric characters.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Include at least one special character.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Use at least one capital letter.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Ensure a minimum length of</mark> <mark style="color:red;"></mark><mark style="color:red;">**13 characters**</mark><mark style="color:red;">.</mark>

{% hint style="info" %}
If you have forgotten your password, you may also refer to [Reset Password from Login Page](personal-settings.md#reset-password-from-login-page) or [Reset Password for Teams](personal-settings.md#reset-password-for-teams)
{% endhint %}



## Notifications

Here, you can enable sound notifications for new incoming messages from your assigned contacts. This will produce a beep sound whenever a new chat arrives.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (211).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
The sound notification will only work when a new incoming message is assigned to you. If you want messages to be automatically assigned to you, refer to the [Auto Assign to User Automation here.](../automations/import-automation-template/auto-assignment-to-user.md)
{% endhint %}
