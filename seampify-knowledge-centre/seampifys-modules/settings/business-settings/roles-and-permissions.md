# Roles & Permissions

Through **Roles & Permissions** Setting, you can create different roles and allow specific permissions to them. Then, use Roles to create Team members and provide them control as per allowed permissions. This is useful to give team members limited control over the Seampify platform and its options.



## Create a Role

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
At Roles & Permissions window, click the **Create Role** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (535).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Define a name for the Role and click **Save**.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (536).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}



## Assign Permission

Once you've created a Role, you'd find it in the tab. Then, to assign permissions, simply click the checkbox next to permission.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (537).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Or select them all by checking the Bulk box.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (538).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



## Editing a Role

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (539).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To edit a Role, click the Menu (three vertical dots) icon, then select **Edit**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (540).png" alt="" width="240"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

It will allow you to change the name. Once edited, click **Save**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (541).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



## Delete a Role

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (539).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To delete a Role, click the Menu (three vertical dots) icon, then select Delete.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (542).png" alt="" width="251"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Then click the **Confirm** button to finalise deleting.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (543).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
