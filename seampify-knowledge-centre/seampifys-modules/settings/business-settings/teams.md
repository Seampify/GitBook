# Teams

Team setting allows you to create user accounts for your employers at Seampify. You can provide them with complete control or limited based on their position. Using their account, your team members can perform different tasks on assigned channels based on their [Department](department.md) and User [Role & Permission](roles-and-permissions.md).

This streamlines the process of managing Channels connections to the different chat platforms. Enabling you and your team to respond to users/contacts in a timely manner and improve user-client relationships for the Business.



## Adding a Team Member

{% hint style="info" %}
You need to [create a Role](roles-and-permissions.md#create-a-role) before adding a member.
{% endhint %}

On the Team window, click **+ Add Member** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (547).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This will open the create user modal with the following fields,

* Full Name
* Email
* Password
* Departments
* Roles
* Max Chat Assignment
* Allow User to SSO login only - Login via Gmail or Microsoft.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (548).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Simply add the information, and once you've added all click **Save**. As soon as you save the Team member it will appear on the page like the below list.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (549).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



## Activate/Deactivate a User

At Seampify, you get the option to activate/deactivate a Team member's account whenever needed. Whether for permanent/temporary reasons.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (550).png" alt="" width="237"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click on the status bar to toggle between Active and Inactive status.



## Search for a Team Member <a href="#search-for-a-team-member" id="search-for-a-team-member"></a>

To quickly find out a team member's account, you can use the Search option.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (551).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Simply enter the name of the member in the Search bar. And if only naming isn't working, then you narrow down the search using the Role option.

## Resetting password for a Team Member's Account

If a team member forgets their password for their Seampify account, the Master User can easily reset it for them. Simply click the three dots under the **Action** column and select **Password**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (552).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Then, enter a new password and click **Save**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (553).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
This step is only able to perform by the Master User. If you wish to reset the password for Master User, kindly contact Seampify's customer support.
{% endhint %}
