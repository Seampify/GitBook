# Chat

The Chat module at Seampify allows viewing user/customer messages sent or received on different chat platforms. Plus, it enables you to communicate with your customers on different channels and provide top-notch customer service without opening too many platforms or inboxes.

Through Chat, you can respond to queries coming from your connected channel - WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram Line, Viber, and/or WeChat.

The Chat module has different sections that allow you to efficiently manage and perform several actions on conversations.
