# Search Options

Using this particular option in the Chat module, you can search, filter, and sort conversations.&#x20;

To search a conversation in chat, simply type your contact's phone number, name or message in the search field.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (295).png" alt="" width="288"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

As you'll do that, it will bring the conversation matching the search query. You can also look for a particular message by switching to the Messages tab.

Furthermore, you can also sort conversations in the Latest or Oldest order. Simply click the![](https://docs.rampwin.com/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FhElFPtMZjXYjDDMBT5q2%252Fuploads%252FcLgzUjMsyZUEJ7NFYGoQ%252FSort%2520Button.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D1143bbbd-cf1f-420a-990b-8868135fd1ad\&width=40\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=67cdcb21\&sv=2)Sorting button and then select either: **Latest First** or **Oldest First**.
