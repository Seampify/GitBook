# Conversations

This section logs the conversation (all incoming and outgoing messages exchanged) between your agents and contacts.&#x20;



To access Conversations, go to **System Logs** then click on the **Conversations** tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (656).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The following fields are included in the message history logs:

* **Agent Name** - The name of the agent registered in your Seampify account at the time of the conversation.
* **Email** - The email of the agent registered in your Seampify account.
* **Department** - The department where the conversation was assigned.&#x20;
* **Customer Name** - The full name of the contact registered in your Seampify account at the time of the message.
* **Contact Number** - The contact's phone number.
* **Chat Initiation Time** - The timestamp of when the conversation was initiated.
* **Assigned At** - The timestamp of when the conversation was assigned to the agent.
* **Avg. First Response Time** - The average time taken by the agent to respond to the first message.
* **Avg. Response Time** - The average response time for the entire conversation from the time it was assigned to the closing time.
* **Closing Time** - The total time taken to close the conversation. If the conversation status remains open, the closing time will display as **0h 0m 0s**.
* **Current Conversation Status** - The status of the conversation at the time you view the report.
* **rating** - The rating provided by the contact for the conversation, if available.
* **review** - Any review or feedback provided by the contact for the conversation, if applicable.
* **Action** - Click <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (650).png" alt="" data-size="line"> to open the conversation window, click <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (651).png" alt="" data-size="line"> to copy the conversation popup link to your clipboard, or click ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (653).png>) to download all messages from the conversation as a CSV file.



## Viewing the Conversations informations

To view conversation details, you must first apply filters. You can filter by:

* **Channel** - Select the channel to view conversations (e.g., WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, or other integrated channels).
* **Date Range** - Select a time frame for the message history: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days and Custom.
* **Department** - Filter conversations by the department they are assigned to.&#x20;
* **Search Bar (Optional)** - Enter a contact's phone number to filter and display all conversations exchanged with that contact.
