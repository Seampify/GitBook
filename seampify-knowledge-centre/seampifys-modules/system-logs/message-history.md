# Message History

This section logs all incoming and outgoing messages exchanged between your WhatsApp number and your contacts.&#x20;



To access Message History, go to **System Logs.** By default, it will loads this Message History window.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (643).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The following fields are included in the message history logs:

* **Customer Name** - The full name of the contact registered in your Seampify account at the time of the message.
* **Customer Number** - The contact's phone number.
* **Customer Email** - The contact's registered email in your Seampify account at the time of the message.
* **Message** - A preview of the message content, either incoming or outgoing.
* **Type** - The type of message sent or received (e.g., text, media, template, interactive and more).
* **Direction** - Indicates if the message is incoming or outgoing.
* **Created At** - The timestamp of when the message was created.
* **Send Status** - The delivery status of the message (e.g., pending, sent, delivered, failed and more).
* **Read Status** - The read status of the message (e.g., viewed, delivered, sent, triggered and more).

{% hint style="info" %}
The viewed status depends on the contact's read status settings. If this setting is disabled, the viewed status will not update.
{% endhint %}

## Message History Filter Functions:

* **Channel** - Choose the channel you want to view the message history for (e.g., WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or other integrated channels).
* **Date Range** - Select a time frame for the message history: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days and Custom.
* **Search Bar** - Filter the message history by entering a contact's name or phone number in the search bar, displaying all message history exchanged with that contact.
