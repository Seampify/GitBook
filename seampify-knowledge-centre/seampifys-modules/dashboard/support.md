# Support

## Overview

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (277).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Total Conversations**: Total number of conversations created on that specified day.

**Total Open Conversations**: Number of conversations that are open on that specified day.

**Total Open Conversations on Bot Side**: Number of conversations that are open on that specified day and handled by bot.

**Total Open Conversations on Agent Side**: Number of conversations that are open on that specified day and handled by agent.

**In Queue**: Number of conversations that are currently in queue, awaiting response from an available agent.

**Total Closed Conversations**: Total conversation closed on that specific day. (Note: Conversation may be created on that specific day or earlier.)

**Total Closed Conversations on Bot Side**: Count of conversations that have been closed by a bot on that specified day.

**Total Conversations closed by Agent**: Count of conversations that have been closed by an agent that specified day.

**Total Abandonments**: Total count of conversations which are automatically been closed after given time from the queue due to agents are busy and not able to respond.

**Total Assigned Conversations**: Number of conversations that have been assigned to an agent on that specified day.
