# Heatmap

## **1. Heatmap Messages**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (754).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Purpose**: This heatmap shows the message activity trends from **yesterday**, grouped hour-by-hour.\
  It helps you identify the peak periods of interaction throughout the day, providing insights into user behavior and engagement levels.

## **2. Heatmap Conversations**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (755).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Purpose**: This heatmap offers an **hour-by-hour breakdown** of conversations or interactions within a specific channel for **yesterday**. It enables you to monitor activity and detect periods of high or low engagement within the channel.

## **Benefits of Heatmaps**

* **Quick Insights**: Easily detect periods of high and low activity.
* **Decision-Making**: Plan actions based on peak hours or trends.
* **Resource Optimization**: Allocate efforts where they are most needed.
