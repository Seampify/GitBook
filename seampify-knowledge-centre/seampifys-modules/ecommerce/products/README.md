# Products

### ✅ Step 1: Navigate to the Products Tab

1. From the left menu, go to **Ecommerce**.
2. Click on the **Products** tab.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (844).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> _Shows product listing page with total number of products._

***

### 🆕 Step 2: Add a New Product (via Meta Commerce)

1. Click the **Add Product** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (845).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. You’ll be redirected to **Meta Commerce Manager** to manage your catalog.

> _Log in to your Facebook account to access Commerce Manager._

3. Once logged in, go to your **Catalog Manager**.
4. Click **Add Items** → select **Add Manually**&#x20;

_Choose manual entry for fast setup._&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
[_Click here to know more about other options to Add Items_](catalog-item-upload-options.md)
{% endhint %}

3. Fill in the required product fields:
   * **Product Name**
   * **Image**
   * **Price & Currency**
   * **Quantity**
   * (Optional) Description or URL
4. Click **Add Item** to save the product to your Facebook catalog.

***

### 🔄 Step 3: Sync Products Back to Seampify

After you’ve added or updated items in Meta:

1. Return to the **Products tab** in Seampify.
2. Click the **Synchronize Products with Facebook** button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (846).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

This ensures all newly added or updated products appear in your Seampify catalog.

***

### 🧾 Product List Overview

Once synced, you’ll see a table like this:

| Field         | Description                         |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 🏷 Name       | Product name from Meta catalog      |
| 💰 Price      | Pricing in MYR, USD, etc.           |
| 🌍 Currency   | Matches Meta settings               |
| 🆔 Product ID | Auto-generated or from Meta catalog |
| 🔗 URL        | Product preview (View Product link) |

> _Products listed in a simple dashboard for review._
