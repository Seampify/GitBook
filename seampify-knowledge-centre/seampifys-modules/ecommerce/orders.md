# Orders

### ✅ Step 1: Navigate to the Orders Tab

1. In Seampify’s left menu, click on **Ecommerce**.
2. Go to the **Orders** tab.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (849).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### 🧾 Step 2: View Order Details

Once you receive orders, each entry will display the following information:

| Field            | Description                                  |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 📆 Date          | When the order was placed                    |
| 👤 Customer Name | The buyer's name from WhatsApp profile       |
| 📱 Phone Number  | Contact number of the customer               |
| 🛒 Items Ordered | List of products added to the basket         |
| 💵 Total Price   | Aggregated cost of all items                 |
| 📦 Order Status  | Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, Completed, etc. |

Click on View all: 1 under Products to **expand details of the item**

***

### ⚠️ No Orders Yet?

If you see:

> _"No orders found"_

This could mean:

* You haven't enabled **"Add to Basket"** in Catalog Settings.
* Customers haven’t submitted any orders yet.
* The products are not visible in the WhatsApp catalog.

To fix:

* Go to **Catalog Settings**
* Enable both toggles:
  * ✅ Show Catalog Icon in Chat
  * ✅ Show “Add to Basket” Button

> _Ensure your setup is complete to receive orders._

***

### 🧠 Tips & Best Practices

* Regularly check your orders tab to respond quickly.
* Use filtered views to stay on top of recent sales.
* Consider setting a webhook in the future for instant order alerts (feature request).
