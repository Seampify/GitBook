# Viewing Form Responses

There are 2 ways to view the form responses:

1. Click on **Form Responses** from the top menu
2. Click on the Responses under Action menu

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (183).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Both ways will direct you to the Form Responses window with some differences:

* Click on **Form Responses** from the top menu - this will show you all responses received for all WhatsApp Flow
* Click on the Responses under Action menu - this will show you the responses received for the dedicated WhatsApp Flow

## Form Responses

Here, you will see the list of responses your Seampify account captured for all forms (using method 1 mentioned above).

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (741).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The following fields are included in the form responses:

* **Name** - The name of the WhatsApp Form.
* **Contact Name** - The full name of the contact registered in your Seampify account at the time of the form submitted.
* **Contact Phone** - The contact's phone number.
* **Response Received At** - The timestamp when the form was submitted.
* **Action** - View the details for each field(s) that was submitted.

If you are using method 2 mentioned above, you will see the list of responses with the details submitted for each field in that particular form.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (150).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The following fields are included in the form responses:

* **Contact Name** - The full name of the contact registered in your Seampify account at the time of the form submitted.
* **Contact Phone** - The contact's phone number.
* **Response Received At** - The timestamp when the form was submitted.
* Other Fields of the Form - View the details for each field(s) that was submitted.

## **Form Responses Filters Functions**

You may perform filters as follow:

*   **Search bar** - Filter by Contact Name or Contact Phone

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (742).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* **Date Range** - Select a time frame for the form responses: Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days and Custom.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (180).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* **Forms** - Select the responses for a specific form. By default, responses for all forms will be displayed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (181).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
