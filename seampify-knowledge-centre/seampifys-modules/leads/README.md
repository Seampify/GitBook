# Leads

**WhatsApp Flows** are a powerful feature designed to help businesses create structured and interactive messaging experiences directly within the WhatsApp platform. This feature enables businesses to engage with their audience through tailored workflows, eliminating the need for users to leave WhatsApp to complete tasks. It is particularly beneficial for form-based solutions, such as:

* Lead generation forms
* Feedback and survey collection
* Sign-in processes
* Browsing product catalogs
* Accessing order history

Businesses can fully customize these flows to align with their specific needs and seamlessly integrate them with existing chatbot solutions. By completing interactions entirely within WhatsApp, brands can enhance user engagement and completion rates, ultimately driving better business outcomes.

## Accessing Lead module

To create a WhatsApp flow, go to the **Leads** module from the left sidebar. Here, you can create or view the WhatsApp Flow and its responses under your WhatsApp account.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (740).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
To be able to publish and sent Flows to your customers you will need to:&#x20;

* [Verify your Business](../../starting-with-seampify/connecting-to-whatsapp-cloud-api/)
* Maintain a [high message quality](../../useful-guides/whatsapp-api-business-account/phone-number-quality-rating/)
{% endhint %}
