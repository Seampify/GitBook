# Understanding 24 Hour Customer Service Window

## 24 Hour Customer Service Window

To drive high-quality conversations between people and businesses, the WhatsApp Business Platform has a 24 Hour Customer Service Window. Whenever a WhatsApp user messages you, this 24-hour timer (customer service window) starts or refreshes if the WhatsApp user messages you again.

When a customer service window is open between you and a user, it falls under the **User-Initiated conversation**, and you can send **any type of message** to the user. If a window is not open between you and the user, you can only send [message template](understanding-whatsapp-message-template/#message-templates) to the user, as message template are the only type that can be sent outside of a customer service window.&#x20;

If you tries to send a message other than a template message outside this 24 Hour Customer Service Window, it will be _failed delivery_.

{% hint style="info" %}
As a reminder, you can only send messages to users who have [opted-in](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/overview/getting-opt-in) to receiving messages from you.
{% endhint %}



## Initiating a conversation beyond 24 hour window

When the business reaches out to the customer that is outside the 24-hour customer service window, its falls under the **Business-initiated conversation**. For a business-initiated conversation, a message template is required.
