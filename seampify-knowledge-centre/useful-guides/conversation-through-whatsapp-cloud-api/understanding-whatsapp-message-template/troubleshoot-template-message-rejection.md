# Troubleshoot Template Message Rejection

### Steps to troubleshoot template message rejection

In this part, we will go over how to troubleshoot rejected template message by checking the reason of rejection from your business manager.&#x20;

**Step 1:**&#x20;

First, go to your "[Business Support Home](https://www.facebook.com/business-support-home)" in your meta business account. Navigate to the WhatsApp account you used for the template message.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/step 1.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2:**

Press " Rejected message templates". Here it will show a list of rejected template messages and their reason for being rejected.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/step 2.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Here is a list of reason of being rejected and ways to resolve it.

If you think you have selected the correct category, you can request for the messages to be reviewed or resubmit the message in the suggested category.</td></tr><tr><td>Shortened URL</td><td>Refrain from using URL shortener for links.</td></tr><tr><td>Misleading</td><td>This can be related to:<br>1. Multi-level marketing Penny auctions<br>2. ICOs and binary options<br>3. Payday loans, pay cheque advances, P2P lending, debt collection and bail bonds<br>4. Diet, weight loss or other health-related products that imply or attempt to generate negative self-perception.<br><br>Please change the content of your messages if they have touch any of the subject above.</td></tr></tbody></table>

### How to appeal for a review of a rejected template

To request for a review on a rejected template, please follow these steps.

**Step 1:**

Select the template message that is rejected that you want to request for a review. Then, click on the "Request review " button.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/step 3.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2:**

Now choose the appeal reason and press "Submit".
