# Tips to send high-quality conversations

You can improve your [quality rating](../../whatsapp-api-business-account/phone-number-quality-rating/) by sending high-quality messages to your customers on Whatsapp Business platform.&#x20;

Here are some tips to help you get a high-quality message out.



### How to create high-quality conversations

There are 3 aspects that needs to be considered when making a high-quality conversations which are expected, timely and relevant.

* **Expected**: Ensure your customers are interested and opted in to receive incoming information from your business on WhatsApp.
* **Timely**: Create conversations that are connected to time-bound topics such as:
  * Seasonal or recurring events related to previous purchases.
  * Products that are recently browsed or purchased in a specific time interval.
* **Relevant**: Share the personalised information with your customers based on previous signal that is related to their interests with a clear call to action such as:
  * Additional information related to the interested product.
  * Add-on products, features or upgrades that is related to previous purchases or services.
