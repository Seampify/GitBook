# How to Apply for Username

{% hint style="info" %}
Please note only eligible account is able to create a username.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

Only [eligible WhatsApp Business Accounts](how-to-apply-for-username.md#eligibility-requirements) can reserve or create a WhatsApp Business Username.

The application process depends on whether your WhatsApp Business Account is using [**Cloud API (WABA)**](how-to-apply-for-username.md#for-waba-accounts) or [**Coexistence**](how-to-apply-for-username.md#for-coexistence-accounts).

## Eligibility Requirements

#### 1. WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)

To claim a WhatsApp username for a **Cloud API (Enterprise)** account, **all** of the following requirements must be met:

* Cloud API Terms of Service (ToS) has been accepted.
* The WhatsApp Business Account has a **2,000+ daily messaging limit (Tier 1 or above)**.
*   Username management is performed through **WhatsApp Manager** or **Meta Business Suite**.

    * Changes made in either platform are automatically synchronized.



#### 2. Coexistence Accounts

For businesses using the **WhatsApp Business App with Coexistence**, the following requirements apply:

* The business must have at least **one active Coexistence SMB WABA**.
* Usernames must be created directly from the **WhatsApp Business App**.

***

### Business Verification Requirement

Business Verification is **mandatory**.

To be eligible:

* The phone number used to claim or reserve a username must belong to a **Business Account that has successfully completed Meta Business Verification**.
* If your business is not verified, you can refer the steps below.

{% content-ref url="../../starting-with-seampify/meta-business-verification.md" %}
[meta-business-verification.md](../../starting-with-seampify/meta-business-verification.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

> **Note**
>
> Completing Business Verification **does not automatically qualify** an account for a username. The account must still satisfy the messaging limit and other eligibility requirements.

### Messaging Limits Requirements

Messaging limits are one of the primary eligibility checks.

Your WhatsApp Business Account must have a **minimum daily messaging limit of 2,000 conversations (Tier 1)**.

{% content-ref url="../broadcasting-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/broadcast-messaging-limit.md" %}
[broadcast-messaging-limit.md](../broadcasting-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/broadcast-messaging-limit.md)
{% endcontent-ref %}

### Additional Eligibility Requirements

Besides Business Verification and Messaging Limits, the following conditions also apply:

* The feature is being released through a **phased rollout**.
* Username reservation officially began on **29 June 2026**.
* The WhatsApp Business Account must be **active**.
* The account must **not be restricted** from messaging due to policy violations.
* The account **cannot be an On-Behalf-Of (OBO)** account.
* The business tax country and business owner country **must not be located in sanctioned regions**.
* Feature availability will continue to roll out progressively across countries throughout **Q3 2026**.

## Business Username Requirements

Before submitting your username, make sure it follows Meta's username requirements.

#### Username Length

Your username must be:

* At least **3 characters**
* Maximum **35 characters**

#### Supported Characters

You can use:

* English letters (`a–z`)
* Numbers (`0–9`)
* Periods (`.`)
* Underscores (`_`)

Your username must contain **at least one English letter**.

#### Username Restrictions

Your username:

* Cannot start with a period (`.`)
* Cannot end with a period (`.`)
* Cannot contain two consecutive periods (`..`)
* Cannot start with `www`
* Cannot end with certain restricted domain extensions
* Is not case-sensitive

#### Examples

**Valid usernames:**

* `JasperMarket`
* `jasper.market`
* `jasper_market`
* `JasperMarketMY`

**Invalid usernames:**

* `.JasperMarket`
* `JasperMarket.`
* `Jasper..Market`
* `www.JasperMarket`
* `123456`

> **Tip:** Choose a username that is easy for your customers to recognise and closely represents your business or brand.

## What Happens After You Apply?

After submitting your preferred username, Meta will check its availability and whether it meets the applicable requirements.

If the username is successfully reserved or approved, it will be associated with your eligible WhatsApp Business phone number.

> **Note:** Username availability and activation may vary depending on Meta's rollout status for your account and region.

***

## What If My Preferred Username Is Already Taken?

WhatsApp Business Usernames are unique.

If your preferred username is unavailable, you will need to choose another available username.

For example, if:

`@JasperMarket`

is unavailable, you could try:

* `@JasperMarketMY`
* `@Jasper.Market`
* `@JasperMarketOfficial`

subject to availability and Meta's requirements.

***

## What If I Cannot See the Username Option?

If you cannot find the Username option in Meta Business Suite, WhatsApp Manager or the WhatsApp Business App, check the following:

#### 1. Check your eligibility

Make sure your WhatsApp Business Account meets Meta's current eligibility requirements.

#### 2. Check Business Verification

Make sure your Business Verification requirements have been completed, where applicable.

#### 3. Check your messaging limit

Your account must meet Meta's applicable messaging-limit requirement.

#### 4. Check your account status

Your WhatsApp Business Account should be active and not restricted because of policy violations.

#### 5. Check the rollout

WhatsApp Business Usernames are being rolled out progressively.

Even if your account meets the general requirements, the feature may not yet be available for your account or region.

***

## Important Notes

* Business Usernames are **optional**.
* A Business Username does **not replace your WhatsApp Business phone number**.
* Your preferred username is subject to availability.
* Meeting the eligibility requirements does not guarantee immediate access to the feature.
* Meta may change the eligibility requirements, username rules and application process as the feature continues to roll out.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>

<summary>Does Business Verification guarantee that I can create a username?</summary>

No.

Business Verification is only one of the eligibility requirements. Your account must also meet Meta's other requirements and have access to the Username feature.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I use any username I want?</summary>

No.

The username must meet Meta's character and formatting requirements and must be available.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I change my username later?</summary>

Username changes are subject to Meta's current rules and availability. If the option is available for your account, you can manage it through the supported Meta interface.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does my Business Username replace my phone number?</summary>

No.

Your WhatsApp Business phone number remains connected to your WhatsApp Business Account. The Business Username provides an additional way for customers to find and connect with your business.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I create a username for every WhatsApp number?</summary>

Eligible business phone numbers can have a Business Username, subject to Meta's eligibility, availability and rollout requirements.

</details>
