# Blue Tick Verification

## **How to Apply for Blue Tick Verification on Your Own (Free Process)**

### **Requirements for Submitting a Blue Tick Verification Request:**

To be eligible for the Blue Tick verification, ensure the following criteria are met:

1. **Use an Approved WhatsApp API Business Account** – Your business must be integrated with WhatsApp API through Seampify.
2. **Enable Two-Step Authentication** – Your WhatsApp Business number must have two-step authentication activated (this is enabled by default for Seampify users).
3. **Verify Your Facebook Business Manager** – Your business should be officially verified within Facebook Business Manager.
4. **Get Your Display Name Approved** – Ensure your business name has been approved by WhatsApp.
5. **Reach a Sufficient Messaging Tier** – You should be at **Tier 2 (10,000 customers/24-hour messaging limit)**.
6. **Operate as a Business** – Only registered businesses can apply; individual accounts are not eligible.
7. **Demonstrate Notability** – Your business must be recognized as a reputable brand with at least **five articles or news features** published in well-known, third-party news sources. **Self-published articles are not accepted.**

***

### **Steps to Apply for Blue Tick Verification via Seampify:**

1.  **Go to Your Facebook Business Settings**

    * Navigate to **Business Settings** → **WhatsApp Accounts** → **WhatsApp Manager**

2.  **Access the Phone Numbers Section**

    * Click on the **Settings** icon next to your business number.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (787).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  **Submit an Official Business Account Request**

    * Open the **Profile** tab and locate the **Official Business Account** section.
    * Click **Submit Request** and fill in the necessary details.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (788).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    * Provide supporting press/media article links showcasing your business’s credibility.

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (789).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4.  **Await Meta’s Review**

    * Meta will assess your application within **1-2 business days**.
    * You can submit up to **five links** from reputable publications (e.g., The Wall Street Journal, Business Insider, Reuters, Wikipedia, etc.) to demonstrate your brand’s public recognition.



***

### **Is Blue Tick Verification Guaranteed?**

It’s important to note that Blue Tick verification differs from WhatsApp API verification. Being approved for WhatsApp API **does not** guarantee an OBA verification.

* WhatsApp grants Blue Tick status only to **highly reputed** businesses that are widely recognized and frequently searched.
* The decision to approve or deny a Blue Tick request is made solely by **WhatsApp** (Meta), not Seampify.

If your application is **rejected**, you can **reapply after 30 days**. During this time, focus on improving your business’s online presence, particularly in reputable news sources, to strengthen your chances of approval.

For further details, refer to WhatsApp's official documentation: [WhatsApp Business Accounts](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/overview/business-accounts#official-business-account).
