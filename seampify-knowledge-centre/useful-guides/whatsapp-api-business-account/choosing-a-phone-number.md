# Choosing a Phone Number

* The phone number used for WhatsApp Business Platform (API) must be valid (can receive SMS or phone call for OTP verification).
* If you want to use a number connected to your personal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business App, you must first delete your account. [Learn how to delete the WhatsApp Account here](how-to-delete-whatsapp-account-on-mobile-app.md)
* Your phone number must not be from any of these [restricted countries](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/support/troubleshooting#country-restrictions)
  * Crimea (+7978)
  * Cuba (+53)
  * Iran (+98)
  * North Korea (+850)
  * Syria (+963)
* Banned WhatsApp numbers cannot be registered unless they are unbanned first. [Contact support](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/support) if your account is banned and you wish to appeal.



## Eligible Phone Numbers for WhatsApp Cloud API:

* Cell phones – can be used as long as the number has not been used for WhatsApp API in the last 6 months
* Landlines (**make sure you are able to to receive voice OTP's**)
* 1-800 or toll-free numbers – **possible as long as the phone number is capable of receiving SMS or voice calls directly.**&#x20;

{% hint style="warning" %}
You will not be able to use numbers that are behind IVR.
{% endhint %}



## Registered Number Limits <a href="#registered-number-limits" id="registered-number-limits"></a>

Business portfolios are initially limited to 2 registered business phone numbers, but this limit can be increased to up to 20 once your [messaging limit ](../broadcasting-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/broadcast-messaging-limit.md#increasing_messaging_limits)has increased to Tier 1 and above.
