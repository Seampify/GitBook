# What happens if my Phone Quality Drops?

To maintain a high-quality in the WhatsApp Business Platform, WhatsApp will enforce any WhatsApp Business Accounts from violating the [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy), the [WhatsApp Commerce Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy) or the [WhatsApp Business Terms of Service](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-terms?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAYnJpZBExcVdFcE43ZHVxa3VKbnlXcAEeTkKxfGn4Qr4icadzykJh9hvvtfrd6ijXwl0Swwsk_euc_5fxIMJ9UOzhtyc_aem_H6sC5t1TRDEsKJrr2rf9ZA).

### What happens?

First, Meta will send you a warning with the information on the policy you violated on your WhatsApp Business Account. \
\
If the Business Accounts continues to violate the WhatsApp Business Terms of Service, such as sending spam messages, misclassify the wrong template messages or send out high-risk policy categories message such as adult content, sale of alcohol and tabacco, drugs, gambling and unsafe supplements, Meta may place messaging restrictions that will gradually increase in duration.\
\
The restrictions are as follows:

* 1 or 3 day block on sending marketing, utility and authentication template messages and adding additional phone numbers to the account.
* 5,7 or 30 day block on sending any messages and adding additional phone numbers to the account.
* An account lock, which is an indefinite block and can only be removed via appealling.
* If the Business Account does not make any changes after multiple warnings and feature limits or blocks, the Business Accounts will eventually be permanently disabled from the WhatsApp Business Platform.

In some cases, WhatsApp will immediately offboard the Business Accounts if they found it to be in violation that causes severe harm to the users such as child exploitation, scams, terrorism or the sale of illegal drugs.

WhatsApp may also limit or offboard your Business Accounts if it is found that your account receives excessive negative feedback from the users.

These violation may be acknowledged or appealed based on the violation type and eligibility.

### How to check on Violations and Appeal them?

When your business account is in violation of a policy, you can find additional detail by reviewing the violation in your Business Support Home.

#### How to Access Account Overview

1. Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and login to your Meta Business Suite.
2. Click All tools in the left-hand menu and select Business Support Home.
3. Click on Account Overview ( the speedometer icon).

In Account Overview, it will show you all the details of the violation such as:

* Summary of policy violation and link to the policy itself.
* Examples of content that is allowed or disallowed based on the policy.
* Whether there is any active restrictions and what will happen if they continue the violation.
* How to avoid future policy violation with helpful links.
* How to appeal.

#### How to appeal violation?

1. From Business Support Home, click on the affected Business Account.
2. From the list of violation, select the violation you want to appeal and click Request Review.
3. A pop up window will appear, fill in the relevant information with supporting details and click on Submit.
4. After submitting, your request will be move to the In Review tab.
5. The appeal review decision will revert back to you usually within 24-48 hour. This decision will either remain Unchanged or be Reversed.

### How to avoid future violations?

* Read the [Commerce and Business Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy).
* Adjust behavior on the platform as soon as possible to avoid additional warnings and/or enforcement actions.
