# User preferences for receiving Marketing Templates

Meta emphasized that people are always in control of their inboxes on WhatsApp. Users can decide how and when they receive messages from businesses through features like message opt-ins.

With **Offers and announcements**, WhatsApp users can indicate their interest level in marketing messages sent from your business, and to stop or resume delivery of marketing messages from your business entirely.

{% hint style="warning" %}
This is a WhatsApp platform feature and is not controlled or visible from Seampify.
{% endhint %}



## Interested/Not Interested feedback

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (90).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

WhatsApp users can use the **Offers and announcements** setting to indicate how interested they are in receiving marketing template messages from your business.

If a user chooses **Not interested**, it can affect [per-user marketing template messaging limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/guides/send-message-templates#per-user-marketing-template-message-limits) between you and the user. Choosing this option also displays the second modal, which gives the user the option to stop delivery of marketing messages from your business.



## Stop/Resume controls <a href="#stop-resume-controls" id="stop-resume-controls"></a>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/whatsapp_business_messages2 copy.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

WhatsApp users can use the **Offers and announcements** setting to stop or resume delivery of marketing template messages from your business.

If you attempt to send a marketing template to a WhatsApp user who has stopped marketing template messages from your business, the API will process the request but not send the message.

{% hint style="success" %}
By default, this settings is turned on. Once Offers and announcements is turned off, business are not allowed to sent Marketing template message. Utility and Authentication template message is still able to sent.
{% endhint %}



## Why This Matters for Your Business

By respecting user preferences and limiting message overload, WhatsApp ensures that your outreach feels more valuable and less like spam. Customers are more likely to engage with your updates when they trust they won’t be overwhelmed with messages.

While this may result in fewer messages reaching certain users during periods of low marketing engagement or reduced inbox activity, your ability to connect with customers when they are most responsive remains unchanged. These updates help businesses focus on **quality interactions over quantity**, leading to higher engagement and stronger customer relationships.



## What Businesses Can Do

To adapt, businesses can:

* **Leverage Utility Messages**: Use approved utility templates (like order updates, appointment reminders, or account notifications) to stay connected without overwhelming users.
* **Encourage Opt‑Ins**: Proactively collect customer permissions to receive updates by integrating opt‑in prompts on your website, in-store, or during conversations.
* **Focus on Timing and Value**: Ensure that any message sent delivers clear value, so customers remain engaged and receptive.

By combining utility templates with proper opt‑in strategies, businesses can continue to reach their audiences effectively—even with stricter messaging controls.
