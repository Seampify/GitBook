# How to Choose a WhatsApp Business Display Name for Your Business

A display name on WhatsApp Business is what your customers see when they interact with your business. To ensure approval and compliance with WhatsApp policies, your display name will undergo a review and must follow specific guidelines. In this tutorial, we'll guide you through the best practices for choosing a compliant WhatsApp Business display name.

## **Business Phone Number Display Name Verification**

### **Conditions for Display Name to Appear**

1️⃣ The **business portfolio** associated with the WhatsApp Business Account must complete **business verification** and being **approved.**\
2️⃣ The **business phone number's name\_status must be APPROVED**.



### **How to Set a Display Name**

You can set a display name when you perform the step under [connecting to WhatsApp Cloud API.](../../starting-with-seampify/connecting-to-whatsapp-cloud-api/)&#x20;

You may also add or edit your business phone number in [**WhatsApp Manager**](https://business.facebook.com/latest/whatsapp_manager)**,** Select Phone Number under Account Tools <br>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (794).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



### **What Happens After Business Verification?**

✅ **Approved:** Your display name is set to **APPROVED**.\
\
❌ **Rejected:** Your display name is set to **DECLINED**. You should review the guidelines, edit the name at WhatsApp Manager.\
\
Once verified, your [messaging limit](../broadcasting-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/broadcast-messaging-limit.md#increasing_messaging_limits) will be increased.
