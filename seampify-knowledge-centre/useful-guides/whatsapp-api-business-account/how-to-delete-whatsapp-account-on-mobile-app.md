# How to Delete WhatsApp Account on Mobile App

To apply for **WhatsApp API Verification**, you must use a number that does not have an account on any WhatsApp apps.

You can use a phone number that is already registered in the Android, iPhone, or Business application versions of WhatsApp.

However, to register this phone number on WhatsApp Business API, you will need to follow the steps below to delete the WhatsApp account associated with that phone number.



## To delete your WhatsApp account

To delete a phone number for WhatsApp Messenger App or WhatsApp Business App on your mobile, follow the below steps:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Open WhatsApp.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Tap [**More options**](https://faq.whatsapp.com/android/troubleshooting/finding-the-more-options-icon) > **Settings** > **Account** > **Delete my account**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Enter your phone number in [full international format](https://faq.whatsapp.com/general/contacts/how-to-add-an-international-phone-number) and tap **DELETE MY ACCOUNT**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Select a reason for why you're deleting your account in the dropdown.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Tap **DELETE MY ACCOUNT**.&#x20;
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="success" %}
Once the WhatsApp account is deleted, you will need to wait for 3 minutes before the phone number is available to register for a WhatsApp Business API.
{% endhint %}



## Deleting your account will: <a href="#deleting_your_account_will" id="deleting_your_account_will"></a>

* Delete your account from WhatsApp.
* Erase your message history.
* Delete you from all of your WhatsApp groups.
* Delete your backup.
* Disable the QR code of your old WhatsApp account.



## Backup your WhatsApp Account

Before deleting your WhatsApp account, it’s crucial to back up your data to prevent any loss. You can find instructions for backing up your account here:

• **iPhone:** [How to Back Up WhatsApp on iPhone](https://faq.whatsapp.com/902477924463699/?helpref=hc_fnav\&cms_platform=iphone)

• **Android:** [How to Back Up WhatsApp on Android](https://faq.whatsapp.com/481135090640375/?cms_platform=android)
