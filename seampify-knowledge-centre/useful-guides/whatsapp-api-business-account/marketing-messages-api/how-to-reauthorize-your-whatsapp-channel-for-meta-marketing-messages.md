# How to Reauthorize Your WhatsApp Channel for Meta Marketing Messages

Starting **30 November 2026**, Meta will restrict marketing messages for WhatsApp Business channels that have not completed the required Marketing Messages authorization.

To avoid interruptions to your WhatsApp marketing campaigns, you will need to reauthorize your existing WhatsApp Business channel through Seampify and accept Meta's updated Marketing Messages API Terms.

This guide will walk you through the reauthorization process.

#### Why is this required?

Meta is introducing updated Marketing Messages capabilities to help businesses improve their WhatsApp marketing campaigns.

By completing the authorization, your business can:

* **Continue sending marketing messages** without interruption due to the new authorization requirement.
* **Access Meta's enhanced marketing capabilities**, subject to account eligibility.
* **Benefit from marketing optimizations** designed to improve campaign performance and customer engagement.

> **Important:** Please complete the authorization before **30 November 2026**. This process is for your existing WhatsApp Business channel. You do not need to create a new WhatsApp number.

***

### Step 1: Access Your WhatsApp Channel

1. Log in to your **Seampify** account.
2. Navigate to **Settings >** [**Channels**.](https://waba.seampify.com/settings/channels)
3. Locate your existing WhatsApp channel.
4. You may see an orange notification informing you that Meta will restrict marketing messages from 30 November.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (910).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 2: Reauthorize Your Channel

1. Locate the WhatsApp channel that requires reauthorization.
2. Click the **three-dot menu (⋮)** at the bottom-right corner of the channel.
3. Select **Reauthorize** from the dropdown menu.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (911).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> **Note:** Please select **Reauthorize**, not Create Channel, to update your existing WhatsApp Business connection.

***

### Step 3: Log In with Facebook

You will be redirected to the WhatsApp Business connection setup.

1. Click **Login with Facebook**.
2. Log in using the Facebook account that has the full control to your existing Meta Business Portfolio and WhatsApp Business Account.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (913).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 4: Review and Accept Meta's Marketing Messages Terms

A Meta authorization window will appear, explaining the connection between your WhatsApp Business Account and Seampify's integration partner.

On this screen, Meta will explain the updated Marketing Messages capabilities, including:

* Communicating with customers at scale.
* Sending messages with marketing optimizations.
* Sharing relevant event activity data with Meta to support marketing optimization.

By proceeding, you will be accepting the applicable **Marketing Messages API for WhatsApp Terms** and other Meta Business Platform terms displayed on the screen.

1. Review the information and terms.
2.  Click **Continue** to proceed.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (915).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> **Note:** The authorization window may display **AdFortless Sdn Bhd**, Seampify's integration partner. This is expected as part of the connection process.

***

### Step 5: Select Your Existing WhatsApp Phone Number

Next, Meta will ask you to select the WhatsApp phone number to connect.

1. Open the **Phone number** dropdown.
2. Search for or locate your existing registered WhatsApp Business number.
3. Select the number marked **Registered**.
4. Click **Next**.

\
![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (916).png>)

> **Important:** Select your existing registered number rather than entering a new phone number.

***

### Step 6: Select Your Existing Business Assets

Meta will ask you to select the business assets you wish to connect to Seampify.

1. Under **Business portfolio**, select your existing business portfolio.
2. Under **WhatsApp account**, select your existing WhatsApp Business Account.
3. Click **Next**.

\
![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (917).png>)

> **Important:** Ensure you select your existing business portfolio and WhatsApp Business Account. Do not create a new account for this reauthorization.

***

### Step 7: Review and Confirm Permissions

Meta will display the permissions requested by Seampify to manage your WhatsApp Business connection.

These permissions may include:

* Managing your WhatsApp accounts.
* Managing and accessing WhatsApp conversations.
* Logging WhatsApp Business events and sharing relevant event data with Meta.

1. Review the requested permissions.
2. Click **Confirm** to authorize the connection.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (918).png>)

***

### Step 8: Complete the Connection

If Meta requests additional verification:

1. Enter the confirmation code sent to your registered verification method.
2. Click **Next** to proceed.

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (919).png>)

Meta will begin connecting your WhatsApp Business Account.

You may see a **Connecting your account** message while the authorization is being processed.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (920).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once the connection is successful, Meta will display a confirmation screen indicating that your account has been connected.

1. Review the connected account details.
2. Click **Continue to feature setup** if prompted.
3. Complete any remaining instructions shown by Meta.\
   ![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (921).png>)

***

### Step 9: Verify Your Marketing Messages Status

Once you have completed the authorization, return to **Seampify > Settings >** [**Channels**](https://waba.seampify.com/settings/channels).

1. Locate your WhatsApp Business channel.
2. Wait for 3 minutes then click **Resync** if the Marketing Messages warning is still displayed.
3. Wait for Seampify to refresh the Marketing Messages status.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (922).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You may see a confirmation message:

**"Marketing Messages status updated"**

***

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### 1. Will my existing WhatsApp number change?

No. You will be reauthorizing your existing registered WhatsApp Business number. There is no need to register a new number.

#### 2. Will my existing WhatsApp conversations be deleted?

Reauthorization is intended to update your existing connection and permissions, not delete your conversations. Please ensure you select the correct existing WhatsApp Business Account and phone number.

#### 3. Is this reauthorization mandatory?

Yes, if you wish to continue sending marketing messages through an affected WhatsApp Business channel after 30 November 2026.

#### 4. Does this affect normal customer service conversations?

The restriction described in this notice specifically concerns marketing messages. Other message categories remain subject to their respective Meta policies and account restrictions.

#### 5. Will there be additional charges?

This authorization process does not itself indicate a new Seampify subscription charge. Existing applicable WhatsApp messaging charges continue to apply.

#### 6. What if I cannot complete the authorization?

Please contact the **Seampify Support Team** with a screenshot of the error or the status displayed on your Channels page. Our team will assist you with the next steps.

***

### Need Assistance?

If you encounter any difficulties during the reauthorization process, please reach out to the **Seampify Support Team**.

We recommend completing the process as early as possible to avoid disruptions to your upcoming WhatsApp marketing campaigns.
