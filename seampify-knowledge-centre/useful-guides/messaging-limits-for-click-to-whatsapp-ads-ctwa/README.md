# Messaging Limits for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA)

Meta has updated its messaging and pricing rules for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) and Facebook Page Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, effective September 28, 2026.

With this update, the free messaging window has been extended from 72 hours to up to 7 days, giving businesses more time to engage with customers without incurring Meta messaging charges.

Below is a guide on how these changes impact businesses using Seampify.

***

## What is CTWA?

Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) are advertisements on Facebook or Instagram that direct customers to your business's WhatsApp number.

When a customer clicks the ad and sends a WhatsApp message, your business can respond, answer enquiries, and follow up with potential customers directly through Seampify.

***

## **What Has Changed?**

*   Previously, Meta provided a **72-hour free messaging window** for eligible conversations initiated through CTWA ads.

    Under the latest update:

    * The free messaging window has been extended from **72 hours to up to 7 days**.
    * Businesses must respond to the customer's initial message within **24 hours** to activate the free-entry window.
    * The 7-day window begins when the business sends its first response.
    * During this period, eligible messages, including marketing, utility, authentication, and service messages, are free of Meta messaging charges.
    * The standard **24-hour customer service window** still applies to free-form messages.

    This gives businesses more time to follow up with potential customers and convert enquiries into sales.

***

### **Important Notes:**

**24-Hour Customer Service Window vs. 7-Day Free Messaging Window**

These two messaging windows serve different purposes.



1. **24-Hour Customer Service Window**

* Opens when a customer sends a message.
* Allows businesses to send free-form messages, such as text, images, and interactive messages.
* Resets whenever the customer sends a new message.
* Once the 24-hour window closes, businesses must use approved WhatsApp message templates to continue messaging.



2. **7-Day Free Messaging Window**

* Applies to eligible conversations initiated through CTWA ads or Facebook Page CTA buttons.
* Activates when the business replies within 24 hours of the customer's initial message.
* Begins from the business's first response and can remain open for up to 7 days.
* Eligible messages sent within this period are free of Meta messaging charges.
* If the 24-hour customer service window has closed, businesses can continue sending approved message templates within the 7-day free window.



Example:

A customer clicks your Facebook ad and sends a WhatsApp message on Monday at 10:00 AM.

Your business replies at 10:05 AM.

* Customer service window: Open until Tuesday at 10:00 AM, unless the customer sends another message.
* Free messaging window: Can remain open until the following Monday at 10:05 AM.
* After Tuesday at 10:00 AM: Approved WhatsApp templates are required unless the customer sends another message.
* After the 7-day window expires: Standard WhatsApp messaging charges apply.



**Messaging Charges After 7 Days**

Once the free-entry window expires, eligible messages will no longer receive the CTWA free messaging benefit.

Messaging charges will follow Meta's current per-message pricing model, depending on the message category and recipient country.

Refer to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform Pricing for more details.



**CTWA Ads Are Not Free**

Although eligible WhatsApp messages can be free for up to 7 days, the advertisement itself is not free.

Businesses will still incur advertising costs when running CTWA campaigns through Meta Ads Manager.<br>

**Eligibility Requirements**

To qualify for the free messaging window:

* The customer must initiate the conversation through a CTWA ad or supported Facebook Page CTA button.
* The customer must use WhatsApp on an Android or iOS device.
* The business must respond within 24 hours of the customer's initial message.

Conversations initiated through ordinary WhatsApp links or unsupported entry points may not qualify for the free-entry benefit.

## **How to Leverage This Update in Seampify?**

#### 1. Maximize Customer Engagement

With up to 7 days of free messaging, businesses have more time to respond to customer enquiries and nurture potential leads.

Ensure your team responds promptly to activate the free messaging window.

#### 2. Optimize Ad Spend

Make full use of the free messaging period to engage with customers who have shown interest in your advertisements.

This can help reduce WhatsApp messaging costs while improving lead conversion opportunities.

#### 3. Automate Responses

Using Seampify's chatbot and automation features, businesses can automatically respond when customers initiate conversations through CTWA ads.

**Important:** If the 24-hour customer service window has closed, only approved WhatsApp message templates can be sent, even when the 7-day free messaging window remains active.

#### 4. Create a 7-Day Drip Campaign

Businesses can build a structured follow-up sequence using Seampify to guide customers from their initial enquiry to conversion.

For example:

| Timeline | Suggested Follow-Up                                                                                            |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Day 1    | Send a welcome message and confirm the customer's interest.                                                    |
| Day 2    | Share product information, services, or FAQs.                                                                  |
| Day 3    | Follow up on the customer's enquiry and offer assistance.                                                      |
| Day 4    | Share testimonials, product benefits, or success stories.                                                      |
| Day 5    | Provide a personalized recommendation or relevant promotion.                                                   |
| Day 6    | Encourage the customer to take action, such as booking an appointment or speaking with a sales representative. |
| Day 7    | Send a final follow-up before the free messaging window expires.                                               |
