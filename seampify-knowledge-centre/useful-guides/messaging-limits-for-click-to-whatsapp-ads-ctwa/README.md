# Messaging Limits for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA)

Meta has updated the pricing and messaging rules for Click-to-WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) and Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, effective **March 1st, 2023**. Below is a guide on how these changes impact businesses using Seampify.

***

## What is CTWA?

Click to WhatsApp Ads (CTWA) is a created ads that can direct your customers to your Business WhatsApp number.&#x20;

***

## **What Has Changed?**

* When a user initiates a conversation through a **Click-to-WhatsApp Ad (CTWA)**, the first **72 hours** of messaging with that user will be **free of charge**.
* Businesses can communicate with users within this **72-hour free window** without incurring [conversation charges.](../conversation-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/whatsapp-conversation-based-pricing/)

***

### **Important Notes:**

1. **Post 72-Hour Messaging Charges:**
   * After **72 hours**, any further conversation with the same user will be **charged** according to WhatsApp's **conversation-based pricing model**.
   * Refer to [WhatsApp's **Conversation Pricing Model**](../conversation-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/whatsapp-conversation-based-pricing/) for details on applicable costs.
2. **CTWA Ads Are Not Free:**
   * While the first conversation is **free**, the **advertisement itself is not free**. Running a CTWA campaign will still incur ad costs.

***

## **How to Leverage This Update in Seampify?**

* **Maximize Customer Engagement**: Since the first 72 hours are free, ensure your business responds promptly to queries and nurtures leads efficiently.
* **Optimize Ad Spend**: Design your ad strategy to make the most of the free messaging window and convert users within this period.
* **Automate Responses**: If using a chatbot, plan interactions effectively, keeping in mind that if you are sending a message beyond the 24-hour live window, you can only sent a template message.
*   **Create Drip Campaign:**&#x20;

    * Build a structured follow-up sequence to guide leads from awareness to conversion within the free 72-hour window. For example:
      * **Hour 0–2:** Send a warm welcome message and confirm their interest.
      * **Hour 12:** Share product highlights, testimonials, or FAQs to build trust.
      * **Hour 24–36:** Offer a personalized promotion, limited-time deal, or free consultation.
      * **Hour 48–60:** Remind them of the benefits and provide a clear CTA (e.g., book now, talk to consultant).
      * **Hour 72:** Send a final nudge before the free window closes, encouraging them to act immediately.

    This ensures consistent touchpoints that keep your brand top-of-mind while maximizing conversions within the free engagement period.



By understanding these updates, businesses using Seampify can better manage their Click-to-WhatsApp Ads and optimize customer interactions while keeping costs under control.

For more details on pricing, visit [WhatsApp’s Conversation-Based Pricing Model](../conversation-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/whatsapp-conversation-based-pricing/).
