# Broadcasting through WhatsApp Cloud API

Beginning May 2, 2022, your businesses can start messaging customers after you complete the [onboarding processes](../../starting-with-seampify/connecting-to-whatsapp-cloud-api/). Business verification and display name review will no longer be needed to get started onto the WhatsApp Business Platform.&#x20;

After sign-up, your business can:

* Respond to unlimited customer-initiated conversations in [24-hour Customer Service Windows.](../conversation-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/understanding-24-hour-customer-service-window.md)
* Broadcast [business-initiated conversations ](../conversation-through-whatsapp-cloud-api/understanding-whatsapp-message-template/)to 250 unique customers in a rolling 24-hour period per phone number.
* Register up to 2 phone numbers.



Learn more about Broadcast Messaging Limit in the next article.
