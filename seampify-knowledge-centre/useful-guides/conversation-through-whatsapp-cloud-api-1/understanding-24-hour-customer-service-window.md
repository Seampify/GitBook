# Understanding 24-Hour Customer Service Window

## 24-Hour Customer Service Window

The WhatsApp Business Platform has a **24-hour customer service window** that opens whenever a customer sends your business a message. Each new customer message resets the 24-hour timer.

During this window, businesses can reply using regular messages or approved message templates.

Once the window expires, businesses can only send [approved message templates](understanding-whatsapp-message-template/#message-templates) until the customer sends another message.

Attempting to send a regular message outside this window will result in a  _failed delivery_.

{% hint style="info" %}
As a reminder, you can only send messages to users who have [opted-in](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/overview/getting-opt-in) to receiving messages from you.
{% endhint %}

## Initiating a Conversation Beyond the 24-Hour Window

When a business contacts a customer outside the 24-hour customer service window, an **approved WhatsApp message template is required**.

Before sending business-initiated messages, ensure the customer has provided the appropriate [opt-in consent](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/overview/getting-opt-in).

To get started, create a message template and submit it to Meta for approval.
