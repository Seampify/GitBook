# Conversations through WhatsApp Cloud API

There are two ways businesses can communicate with customers through the WhatsApp Business Platform:

1. **User-initiated conversations** — When a customer sends a message to the business.
2. **Business-initiated conversations** — When a business starts a conversation with a customer.

Each follows different messaging rules depending on the **24-hour customer service window**.

### 1. User-Initiated Conversations

A user-initiated conversation begins when a customer sends a WhatsApp message to your business.

This opens a **24-hour customer service window**, during which your business can reply using regular messages or approved message templates.

Every time the customer sends a new message, the 24-hour window resets.

Once the window expires, your business can only send an approved message template until the customer messages again.

### 2. Business-Initiated Conversations

A business-initiated conversation occurs when your business reaches out to a customer without an active 24-hour customer service window.

For business-initiated messaging, an **approved WhatsApp message template is required**.

These templates may fall under Marketing, Utility, or Authentication categories, depending on the message's purpose.

Businesses must also obtain the appropriate customer opt-in before initiating messages.

### 3. Understanding the Difference

|                  | User-Initiated                    | Business-Initiated             |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Who starts it?   | Customer                          | Business                       |
| 24-hour window   | Opens when the customer messages  | May not be open                |
| Regular messages | Allowed within the window         | Not allowed outside the window |
| Message template | Optional within the window        | Required outside the window    |
| Pricing          | Based on applicable message rules |                                |
