# Tips to Send High-Quality Conversations

You can improve your [quality rating](https://seampify.gitbook.io/seampify/useful-guides/whatsapp-api-business-account/phone-number-quality-rating) by sending high-quality messages to your customers through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Here are some tips to help maintain a good quality rating.

### How to Create High-Quality Conversations

There are three key aspects to creating high-quality conversations: **Expected, Timely, and Relevant**.

* **Expected:** Ensure customers have opted in and are interested in receiving messages from your business on WhatsApp.
* **Timely:** Send messages at appropriate times, such as:
  * Seasonal or recurring events related to previous purchases.
  * Follow-ups on recently viewed or purchased products.
* **Relevant:** Share personalised information based on your customers' interests, with a clear call to action, such as:
  * Additional information about products they are interested in.
  * Related products, features, or upgrades based on previous purchases.
