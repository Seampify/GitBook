# Reset Your Seampify Password

If you’ve forgotten your password, there are 3 ways to reset it and regain access to your account:

* [Reset Password from Login Page](reset-your-seampify-password.md#reset-password-from-login-page)
* [Reset Password from Personal Settings](reset-your-seampify-password.md#reset-password-from-personal-settings)
* [Reset Password for Teams](reset-your-seampify-password.md#reset-password-for-teams)

## **Reset Password from Login Page**

### _**Step 1: Access the Forgot Password Page**_

*   Open your web browser and visit [**https://waba.seampify.com/**](https://waba.seampify.com/auth/login)

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-11 160927.png" alt="" width="268"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
* Click on the Forgot Password button and it will direct you to the [Forgot Password Page](https://waba.seampify.com/auth/forgot-password)

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-10 142442.png" alt="" width="355"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 2: Enter Your Registered Email Address**_

On the Forgot Password page:

* Enter the "E**mail Address"** associated with your Seampify account.
* Click the **"Send reset link"** button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-10 142753.png" alt="" width="357"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 3: Recovery Notification**_

* After submitting your email address, you will be directed to a **Password Recovery** page.
* This page confirms that a password recovery email has been sent to your registered email address.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-10 143026.png" alt="" width="354"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 4: Check Your Email for a Password Reset Link**_

* Open your email inbox and locate the email from **Seampify** with the subject line **"Reset Your Password."**
* In the email:
  * Click the **"Reset Password"** button.
  * Alternatively, you can copy and paste the provided link into your web browser.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-11 161844.png" alt="" width="353"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 5: Access the Reset Password Page**_

* After clicking the link, you will be redirected to the **Reset Password** page, where you can create a new password.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-10 015321.png" alt="" width="332"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 6: Create a New Password**_

On the password reset page:

* Enter a new password following these guidelines:
  * <mark style="color:red;">Use alphanumeric characters.</mark>
  * <mark style="color:red;">Include at least one special character.</mark>
  * <mark style="color:red;">Use at least one capital letter.</mark>
  * <mark style="color:red;">Ensure a minimum length of</mark> <mark style="color:red;">**13 characters**</mark><mark style="color:red;">.</mark>
* Confirm the new password by re-entering it.
* Click the **"Reset My Password"** button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-10 024433.png" alt="" width="295"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 7: Password Reset Success**_

* After successfully resetting your password, you will see a confirmation message.
* Click the **"Go back to login"** button to return to the login page.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-10 150131.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## **Reset Password from Personal Settings**

If you wish to reset your password, you may perform this at Personal Settings section.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Go to Settings module and select Security & Password

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (368).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Enter your old password on top, then enter a new password following these guidelines:

* <mark style="color:red;">Use alphanumeric characters.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Include at least one special character.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Use at least one capital letter.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Ensure a minimum length of</mark> <mark style="color:red;">**13 characters**</mark><mark style="color:red;">.</mark>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Click Change Password once done.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## **Reset Password for Teams**

As the master user, you are able to reset the password for your team member.

{% hint style="warning" %}
This step is only able to perform by the Master User. If you wish to reset the password for Master User, kindly contact Seampify's customer support.
{% endhint %}

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Go to Settings module and select Teams

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (371).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Select the 3 dots icon <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (372).png" alt="" data-size="line"> under Action

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (373).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Enter a new password following these guidelines:

* <mark style="color:red;">Use alphanumeric characters.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Include at least one special character.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Use at least one capital letter.</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">Ensure a minimum length of</mark> <mark style="color:red;">**13 characters**</mark><mark style="color:red;">.</mark>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (374).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Click Save.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## **Log In with Your New Password**

Once you have successfully reset your password, you may log in with your new password

* Go to the login page: [Seampify Login Page](https://waba.seampify.com/auth/login).
* Enter your **email address** and your **new password.**
* Click the **"Login"** button to access your account.
