# Sign up your Seampify account

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/z1mLZswPD84" %}

## Sign Up Your Account

To create an account with Seampify , visit the Seampify registration page.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you already have an Account, click the link [Login Seampify Account](login-to-your-seampify-account.md)
{% endhint %}



### _**Step 1: Fill out the Registration Form**_

You will be greeted with the registration form as shown below to begin the sign-up process.

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-04 141632.png" alt="" width="243">

Fill in the following information accordingly:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
Enter Your Email Address

<mark style="color:red;">e.g: abc@gmail.com</mark>

{% hint style="warning" %}
This email address used here will be the Master User of your account.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Create a Password

* <mark style="color:red;">Alphanumeric characters</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">At least one special character</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">At least one capital letter</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">A minimum length of 13 characters</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">e.g: Abc@123\_123</mark>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Select Country

Choose your country from the dropdown menu
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Phone Number

&#x20;Enter your phone number
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
Enter the **"Register"** button&#x20;

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (397).png" alt="" data-size="original">
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### _**Step 2: Confirmation Page and Email Verification**_

After registering, you will be directed to a confirmation page. This page informs you that a verification email has been sent to your email address.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-09 213404.png" alt="" width="239"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 3: Verify Your Email Address**_

To verify your email:

1. Open your email inbox.
2. Locate the verification email from **support@seampify.com**.
3. Click the **"Verify Email Address"** button in the email.
   * Alternatively, copy and paste the verification link provided in the email into your web browser.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-09 213808.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 4: Verification Success**_

* Once your email is verified, you will be redirected to a success page.
* Click the **"Go back to login"** button to return to the login page.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-09 214106.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### _**Step 5: Log In to Your Account**_

* On the login page:
  1. Enter your registered email address.
  2. Enter your password.
  3. Click the **"Login"** button.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-12-09 214536.png" alt="" width="235"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



### _**Step 6: Access the Seampify Dashboard**_

* After successfully logging in, you will be redirected to the Seampify Dashboard.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (367).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* From here, you can explore and utilize the features available. But, you will need to [connect to a channel first.](../connecting-to-whatsapp-cloud-api/)
