# Setup Business Profile

## 1. Go to Manage Profile&#x20;

* Navigate to **Settings > Manage Profile** under Cloud API&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (575).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## 2. Update Details&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (570).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Here, you can change your Profile Picture, Address, Description, Email, Website and Business Vertical from the available options:

* Automotive Beauty
* Spa and Salon
* Clothing and Apparel
* Education
* Entertainment
* Event Planning and Service
* Finance and Banking
* Food and Grocery
* Public Service
* Hotel and Lodging
* Medical and Health
* Non-profit Professional Services
* Shopping and Retail
* Travel and Transportation
* Restaurant
* Other

Once done, click **Save Changes**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Accepted file type for profile picture: JPG

Accepted dimensions for profile picture: 800px x 800px or 1200px x 1200px
{% endhint %}
