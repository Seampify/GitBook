# Setting up Payment Method

{% embed url="https://youtu.be/DdZSXljEuXU" %}

This page provides a step-by-step guide to help you configure a payment method in WhatsApp Manager. It ensures smooth and secure transactions by guiding you through adding a payment method.

## **Step 1: Access Payment Settings**

1. Go to [**Meta Business Suite**](https://business.facebook.com/latest/settings/profiles)
2. Navigate to **WhatsApp Accounts > Settings >** click on **Payment Settings**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (597).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 2: Add a Payment Method

1. Click on **Add Payment Method**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (592).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Fill in the required information:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (593).png" alt="" width="545"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="warning" %}
Note: Choose US Dollars for Currency
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (595).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3.  Fill up card details<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (166).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    Click **Save** and **Done**.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (168).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4. Provide your **Tax Information**:

*   Go to Business Info and click on **Edit.**<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (169).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
*   Fill up the informations and indicate that this is for **Business purposes** by selecting **Yes**<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (170).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Step 3: Finalize the Payment Method

Ensure your payment card is displayed under the Payment Settings once you've completed the setup.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (598).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

If the card does not appear:

* Return to **Payment Settings** and manually select your card as the **default payment method**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (171).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
