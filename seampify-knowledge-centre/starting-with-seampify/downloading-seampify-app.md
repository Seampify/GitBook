# Downloading Seampify App

Download the app in your mobile phone by searching for **Seampify** on App Store or scan the QR code to download accordingly:

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (695).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

| App for IOS                                                                 | App for Android                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (165).png" alt="" data-size="original">  | <img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (164).png" alt="" data-size="original">                                 |
| [Click to Apple Store](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/seampify/id6504795020) | [Click to Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.seampify.app\&hl=en\&pli=1) |

1. Once the download is complete, tap **Open** to launch the Seampify app.
2. Follow the on-screen instructions to log in or register for your Seampify account.
3. To receive instant notifications:
   * Grant the app permission to send notifications when prompted.
   * Ensure notifications are enabled in your phone’s settings.
