# Onboarding WhatsApp Business app users (Coexistence)

You can now choose to onboard with Seampify with an existing Whatsapp Business App account which would allow you to use both WhatsApp Business App and Seampify. Both sides will be synced for their message chat history.&#x20;

During onboarding with Seampify, you have the option to choose between connecting with an existing WhatsApp Business App or with a new phone number.

{% hint style="warning" %}
&#x20;Please note: Ensure your WhatsApp Business App is updated to the latest version to avoid issues during the onboarding.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="danger" %}
Before that, you need to link your WhatsApp Business App number into your business portfolio, [follow the step mentioned here](../../useful-guides/messaging-limits-for-click-to-whatsapp-ads-ctwa/connecting-waba-number-to-facebook-page-to-perform-ads.md)
{% endhint %}

## Things to note about Coexistence

### Requirements <a href="#requirements" id="requirements"></a>

* the number must be using in WhatsApp Business app version **2.24.17** or higher.
* the phone number country code must be supported. Unsupported countries: Nigeria\
  and South Africa



### Limitations <a href="#limitations" id="limitations"></a>

* If your business phone number has connected with a partner in the past and still shares the previous credit line, you may see an error when attempting to switch to a new partner. Contact us to resolve the error.<br>
*   Your business phone number can link up to 4 WhatsApp "companion" clients to your WhatsApp Business app account on other devices (described as "[linked devices](https://faq.whatsapp.com/378279804439436/?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAYnJpZBExZXV1b2JqczRsMEplZ2d3ZHNydGMGYXBwX2lkATAAAR6SerJnt8tsYD1_fSL-9MiUxjCYQWAGRhS4sHCY8DrZuQcWIYBamYE2JHYcAg_aem_lt2Poh4Lk-R3DJLBWDuPyA)" in our Help Center).<br>

    All companion clients are supported, except for [WhatsApp for Windows](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1317564962315842/?cms_platform=windows-desktop\&fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAYnJpZBExZXV1b2JqczRsMEplZ2d3ZHNydGMGYXBwX2lkATAAAR4kGq1aViydVLW1sR1Spb_s_eBFPtt76qQOQvfSlT7o4TL7PEyxjyFBJZZiZg_aem_XrsAy9MaZpoKLLYYfBnQrQ) and [WhatsApp for WearOS](https://faq.whatsapp.com/564431798835071/?fbclid=IwZXh0bgNhZW0CMTEAYnJpZBExZXV1b2JqczRsMEplZ2d3ZHNydGMGYXBwX2lkATAAAR7Cmvh0unn5ytZqJ_aR-8z-dmF1qrkRhXcvobkqdOz0Km8QTTPWRdXDf9Ej8g_aem_0YaHZkqcYylvo7fhmgqIww).<br>

    Once you have onboards to Cloud API with an existing WhatsApp Business app account and number (Coexistence), all companion apps will be unlinked from the account, and the business can then re-link any supported companion apps.<br>
* If your customers uses an unsupported companion Whatsapp device/app to message your Coexistence business number, they can still sent the messages and seen through the WhatsApp Business app but it won't show the messages on Seampify as Meta does not sync those messages to a Cloud API platform. <br>
* If you sends a message through the Coexistence business number and the customer viewed in an unsupported companion WhatsApp device/app, they won't be able to see the message. Instead, it will appear with a placeholder text, instructing them to view the message in their primary WhatsApp device.



### Pricing <a href="#pricing" id="pricing"></a>

After you have onboarded as Coexistence business number, messages sent out via the WhatsApp Business app will continue to be free, but messages sent via API through Seampify will be subject to [Cloud API pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing/).

See Meta's [API Solutions for WhatsApp Business App Users](https://developers.facebook.com/resources/API-solutions-for-WhatsApp-Business-App-users.pdf) pricing explainer PDF for breakdowns of common pricing scenarios.



### Customer service window <a href="#customer-service-window" id="customer-service-window"></a>

[Customer service windows](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/guides/send-messages#customer-service-windows) will only be opened when a WhatsApp user messages your  business number who is already onboarded onto Cloud API as Coexistence. \
\
If your customer messages your business number just prior to the business number being onboarded onto Cloud API, you can only respond with a template message through Seampify, since no customer service was opened. \
\
If your customer messages your business number after it has been onboarded onto Cloud API as Coexistence, a customer service window will be opened as normal, and the business can then respond with a non-template message through Seampify.

{% hint style="success" %}
The customer service window is not limited in the WhatsApp Business app. This means, you may use the WhatsApp Business App to message your customer even it is beyond the 24 hour Customer Service Window and it is not subject to Cloud API pricing.
{% endhint %}
