# How to check if you are an Admin of your business portfolio

To have full access to your business portfolio, you need to be an Admin. Here is a guide to check for your permissions.

**Step 1:** Login to your Business Suite.

Go to your settings and access People under Users.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (832).png" alt="" width="213"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Check the list and find your name. If you have Full Control under Business Portfolio Access, you are the Admin of the business portfolio. If you only have partial access, you are limited on the things you can do in the business portfolio.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/business portfolio5.PNG" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### How to grant Admin Access.

You can grant people Admin access to the business portfolio. First, press the 3 dots at the left side.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/business portfolio5 (1).PNG" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Go into **Edit business portfolio permissions**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (835).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can check **Manage** under Full Control to grants access to the user. Click **Save** once done.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (836).png" alt="" width="373"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
