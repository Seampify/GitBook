# Create a Business Portfolio

Facebook business portfolio is a place where user's can access and monitor all their META products for their business i.e. Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, ad accounts, product catalogues and other business assets together in one place. You can access this by going to the link [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com).&#x20;

### Things to take note:

* You will need a personal Facebook account - This account is needed for you to login to your Business Suite or Business Manager which is where you will create your business portfolio.
* You can create up to 2 business portfolio maximum for each account. This is not the limit of business portfolio that you can own.

## Steps to create a Business Portfolio

**Step 1: Go to your Business Suite.**\
Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com) in a desktop browser and login to your personal Facebook account. If you do not have a Facebook account, you can create one here.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you already have access to Business Portfolio, you will not see this page and you can skip to Step 2.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (830).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Step 2: Click on **Create a business portfolio** from the left menu bar.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (831).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3: Fill in your business details.**

* **Business Portfolio Name** - This name should match the name of your business and cannot contain any special character.
* Your **Name**.
* Your **Business Email** - META will contact you using this email address about your business. Your customers will not see this email.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (829).png" alt="" width="554"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4: click Crate once done.**\
After creating your business portfolio, you will receive an email to confirm your business.
